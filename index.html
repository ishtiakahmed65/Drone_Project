Reading the demonstration

What the motion means.

Fault ≠ lower RPM

A controller can hold speed while imbalance increases vibration. The fault modes emphasize local vibration; maneuvers alter the rotor speed distribution.

Tip motion, stable center

Arm deflection follows an illustrative cantilever shape. Real mode shapes depend on joints, stiffness, sensor position and frequency. The blue IMU follows the whole frame.

Not a measured spectrum

The plotted signals are synthetic and bypass real sensor filtering. The linked ADXL335-CY board lists a 50 Hz bandwidth; a 3,700 rpm rotor has a 61.7 Hz fundamental. Sample rate and bandwidth must be checked before interpreting recorded flight data.
