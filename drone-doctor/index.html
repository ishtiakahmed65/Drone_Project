LIVE MONITORING

Connect to base station

↗

Connect either to a paired Bluetooth Classic SPP base station or directly to the LoRa board over USB. The website only receives status data; it cannot start motors or control flight.

Bluetooth requires pairing first. For USB, connect the LoRa board and choose its serial port. Live serial connections require desktop Chrome or Edge over HTTPS or localhost; they are not supported in most mobile browsers.
